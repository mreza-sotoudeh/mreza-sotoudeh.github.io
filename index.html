01 About

Models first, then mechanisms.

I am a B.Sc. student in Mechanical Engineering at Sharif University of Technology, focused on dynamics, control, and mechatronics. My projects range from machine design and multibody dynamics to CFD, finite element analysis, and physics-informed neural networks.

I interned in industry, evaluating machine performance and developing a digital readout concept for a milling machine, and I have been a teaching assistant for several mechanical engineering courses. I plan to continue to graduate research in mechatronics and intelligent mechanical systems.
